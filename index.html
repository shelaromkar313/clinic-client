Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding appointments, session counts, safety, and procedures at our Thergaon clinic.

Please contact our clinic desk directly at +91 98340 80515 for current consultation fees and slot availability.

How many sessions are typically required for Laser Hair Removal?

Most patients require between 6 to 8 sessions spaced approximately 4 to 6 weeks apart. This is because hair grows in distinct phases (Anagen, Catagen, Telogen), and the laser only targets follicles actively in the Anagen (growth) phase. Significant hair thinning and slowed regrowth are typically visible after just 2-3 sessions.

How soon can I see results for hair fall treatments?

A reduction in excessive daily hair shedding is usually noticed within 4 to 6 weeks of starting medical treatment and scalp therapy. Visible improvement in hair shaft thickness, baby hair growth along the parted lines, and increased density are typically achieved between 3 to 4 months as follicles complete their natural growth cycles.

Is PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) or GFC therapy safe?

Yes, both PRP and GFC are extremely safe because they are 100% autologous—derived entirely from your own blood. There is zero risk of allergic rejection or foreign body reaction. Dr. Rohini performs each session with sterile, single-use micro-needles and cold anesthesia so you experience minimal discomfort.

You can book in under a minute by clicking any "Book Appointment" button on this website, sending a message directly to our official WhatsApp (+91 98340 80515), or calling our reception during clinic hours (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM). Walk-ins are also welcome, though prior appointment is recommended to minimize waiting time.

What is the typical treatment timeline for stubborn acne and pigmentation?

Active inflammatory acne typically responds within 1 to 2 weeks, with significant clearing over 4 to 8 weeks. For stubborn post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation or melasma, a structured protocol of 3 to 5 chemical peels or Q-switched laser sessions combined with strict sun protection yields gradual, long-lasting skin clarity over 2 to 3 months.
