Dr. Rohini Murade Patil founded Dr. Rui's Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic in 2015 with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between high-end cosmetology and deeply ethical medical dermatology. In an era where skin concerns are frequently met with aggressive sales of standard packages, Dr. Rohini's approach remains rooted in personalized diagnostics and careful listening.

"Every patient who walks into our clinic brings a unique skin story and personal concerns," says Dr. Rohini. "Whether it is an adult struggling with stubborn acne scars, a young professional experiencing sudden hair fall, a child with eczema, or someone recovering from skin burns, our duty is to restore not just their physical skin barrier, but their inner happiness and peace of mind."

Patients throughout Thergaon, Wakad, Hinjewadi, and Pimpri-Chinchwad consistently highlight Dr. Rohini's patient and detailed explanations—ensuring every individual fully understands the root cause of their condition and how each step in their treatment functions.
